Our ethical line

We do not access another person's accounts, phones or devices without lawful authority, and we do not guarantee recovery of funds or a particular outcome. A legitimate investigation should not require your password, private key or wallet seed phrase. Any professional licence or credential should be independently verified with the relevant issuing authority.
