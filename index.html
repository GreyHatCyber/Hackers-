What we do

Focused digital support

Each engagement starts with a clear scope and an assessment of what can be established from the available evidence.

⌘

Authorised security assessments

Security reviews and penetration testing for systems you own or are explicitly authorised to test, with findings documented for remediation.

◎

Blockchain transaction analysis

Review public blockchain records, transaction hashes and wallet movements to organise leads and prepare evidence for relevant platforms or authorities.

▤

Digital evidence support

Structured timelines, evidence inventories and incident documentation to help clients communicate clearly with service providers and investigators.

⌕

Open-source research

Lawful research using publicly available information for due diligence, fraud awareness and verification of digital claims.

◇

Incident guidance

Practical next steps for account compromise, suspicious transfers and digital-security incidents, including reporting and account-protection guidance.

↗

Case documentation

Chronological case logs, evidence references and progress summaries that distinguish confirmed facts from unverified leads.

How it works

A transparent process

01 · Initial review

Share a summary and only the information needed to understand the issue. Do not send passwords, seed phrases or private keys.

02 · Scope and evidence

Agree on authorised activities, costs, deliverables and evidence-handling expectations before work begins.

03 · Findings and next steps

Receive a clear summary of findings, limitations and practical next steps. Results and recovery cannot be guaranteed.

Important: Blockchain analysis can identify public transaction paths, but it cannot by itself reverse a transfer or guarantee that funds can be recovered. Never pay anyone who promises guaranteed recovery, and never share wallet seed phrases or private keys.
Our principles

Responsible, evidence-led work

We aim to communicate clearly, preserve records, respect privacy and operate only within lawful authorisation. Any claimed licence, registration or professional credential should be independently verified through the issuing authority.
