A careful, structured approach

Make decisions with a clearer picture.

Digital investigations can be complex. We focus on organising the available information, documenting limitations and identifying responsible next steps.

  • Review scope, consent and available evidence
  • Document relevant findings and their sources
  • Communicate limitations in plain language
  • Provide practical next steps and reporting options
How we work

Clear steps. No false promises.

A transparent process helps clients understand what is being reviewed, what it may establish and what remains uncertain.

STEP 01

Initial assessment

Share a high-level description of the incident and the type of help you need. Never send passwords, private keys or wallet seed phrases.

STEP 02

Scope and evidence

Agree on authorised activities, deliverables, costs and evidence handling before any work begins.

STEP 03

Findings and next steps

Receive a documented summary of findings, limitations and practical options for further reporting or follow-up.

Important: Blockchain analysis cannot by itself reverse a transaction, and no investigation can guarantee that assets will be recovered. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed results or requesting your wallet seed phrase or private keys.
