Advisories, scam alerts and firm updates

Short, practical updates from our analysts on security threats, fraud patterns and safety guidance. Dates and examples below are editorial sample content and should be verified and updated before being presented as confirmed live incidents or company announcements.

12 Oct 2026
Scam alert

Fake “fund recovery” agents impersonating investigators on social media

Fraudsters may copy the names and logos of investigation firms, then approach people who have already lost money and demand additional fees. Be cautious of unsolicited messages, verify identities independently, and never share wallet seed phrases or private keys. This site should not claim it never initiates contact or never accepts crypto payments unless that is an adopted and accurate company policy.

28 Sept 2026
Advisory

Address-poisoning attacks on stablecoin users

Attackers may send small transactions from wallet addresses that resemble a recipient's address, hoping the victim copies the wrong one from transaction history. Verify the full destination address using a trusted source before every transfer, and consider a small test transaction when appropriate.

3 Sept 2026
Firm news

Blockchain forensics capability update

Blockchain investigation workflows can involve reviewing public transaction records across supported networks. Any specific claim about team growth, supported chains or service capacity should be confirmed by the company before publication.

14 Aug 2026
Guidance

What to do in the first 48 hours after an investment scam

Stop sending money, preserve messages and transaction identifiers, contact your bank or payment provider promptly, notify the exchange involved, and report the incident to the appropriate authorities. Beware of follow-up messages promising guaranteed recovery for an advance fee.

21 Jul 2026
Advisory

Deepfake voice calls used in urgent payment requests

Staff should verify unusual or urgent payment requests using a known phone number and an independent second approver. Do not rely on caller ID or a familiar-sounding voice alone.

30 Jun 2026
Scam alert

“Hire a hacker” adverts promising unauthorised account access

Offers to break into someone else's phone, email or social media account may be unlawful and are often used to take payment without delivering anything. Use only authorised security testing and legitimate account-recovery channels.

18 May 2026
Firm news

Security awareness and phishing-simulation guidance

Phishing simulations should be authorised, proportionate, designed to educate participants, and handled with suitable privacy protections. Confirm any specific programme availability or team capacity before advertising it as a current company offering.

9 Apr 2026
Guidance

Checklist: securing email accounts with passkeys

Where supported, passkeys and hardware security keys can help resist phishing. Review account recovery methods, enable multifactor authentication, keep recovery details current, and remove unfamiliar sessions and connected apps.
