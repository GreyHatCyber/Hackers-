Last updated: October 20, 2026

This page describes general privacy principles for enquiries sent to Zenith Hackers Intelligence. Please replace this template with a policy reviewed for your actual business practices before collecting personal information.

Information you provide

If you contact us, you may choose to provide your name, contact details and a description of your enquiry. Do not send passwords, wallet seed phrases, private keys or unnecessary sensitive information.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess whether a request is within scope, communicate about requested services and maintain appropriate records.

Sharing and retention

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purpose or legal obligations. It should not be disclosed to others except with permission or where legally required.

Security and your choices

No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you submitted, contact Support@Zenithhackers.com.

This is a starter template, not legal advice. Update it to accurately reflect your data practices and applicable law.
